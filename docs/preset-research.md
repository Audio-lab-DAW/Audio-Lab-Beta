# AudioLab preset library: research and how the presets are built (part 1, the synths; part 2, the other instruments; part 3, the effects)

Written for parts 2 (the other instruments) and 3 (the effects) of the preset task, and for anyone tuning presets later. The release is v0.197 (451 presets in 16 instruments, 229 before). Nobody has listened to these presets: they come from published recipes, then measurement.

## 1. What makes a good preset (sources)

- **Playable straight away, shows what the synth can do.** Urs Heckmann (u-he): good presets let people "make music right away"; Reveal Sound: presets "should reveal the potential of the synthesiser". Both say quality matters more than quantity. [MusicRadar roundup of developers](https://musicradar.com/news/tech/must-a-synth-come-with-a-large-supply-of-good-presets-639042)
- **Curate.** Serum 1 shipped about 450 presets cut down from about 1,000 submitted (Steve Duda, same article). Serum 2: 626 presets ([Xfer](https://www.xferrecords.com/products/serum-2)). u-he counts: Diva 1354, Hive 2 2420, Zebra2 551 ([u-he](https://new.u-he.com/support/faq/product-info/preset-count)). Vital's own factory set is small (43 "Factory", 235 with the packs, [Vital forum](https://forum.vital.audio/t/number-of-presets-and-waveforms-in-vital-plus/5164)).
- **Bread and butter plus showcase.** Massive X designers aimed at usable "bread and butter" sounds and a few extreme ones. [NI blog](https://blog.native-instruments.com/massive-x-lab-sound-design/)
- **Not drowned in effects; fits a mix.** Factory presets "are designed to sell the synth" and often need stripping down ([Gearnews](https://www.gearnews.com/?p=259059), [MusicRadar](https://musicradar.com/how-to/how-to-strip-back-a-synth-preset-to-make-it-more-usable)); leave room in the top end, use filter envelopes sparingly ([MusicRadar](https://musicradar.com/tuition/tech/10-ways-to-design-better-synth-patches-634900)).
- **Init preset first**: one saw, filter open, gate envelope, no effects, no glide ([Gearnews](https://www.gearnews.com/?p=259059)).
- **Naming** (Steinberg HALion preset guideline): English, no artist or song names, no leading numbers, no category word in the name when the browser already shows the category. [Steinberg](https://steinbergmedia.github.io/halion-script-api/HALion-Tutorials-Guidelines/pages/HS-Preset-Guideline.html)
- **Categories**: Vital uses Bass, Lead, Keys, Pad, Percussion, Sequence, Experiment, SFX, Template ([Vital forum](https://forum.vital.audio/t/categories-for-arps-brass-etc/6362)); NKS advice: a small fixed vocabulary, no synonyms ([NI community](https://community.native-instruments.com/discussion/comment/46421)).
- **Gain staging**: the only published numbers found are Steinberg's (single note at full velocity: basses and leads near -1 to 0 dB, polyphonic pads about -6 dB so chords fit). Users complain about hot or uneven presets ([KVR, Diva levels](https://www.kvraudio.com/forum/viewtopic.php?p=4900736); [KVR](https://www.kvraudio.com/forum/viewtopic.php?p=8009867)).
- **Performance controls**: Steinberg: always assign the mod wheel; u-he soundsets: every preset uses at least one performance control; vibrato is the usual mod wheel job, also filter or wavetable position ([u-he Automata readme](https://u-he.com/downloads/manuals/soundsets/diva/Automata_ReadMe.pdf), [MusicRadar](https://www.musicradar.com/how-to/how-to-design-an-expressive-synth-patch-for-performance)).
- **Psytrance packs**: Elements Psytrance (Serum), 100 presets: Leads 45, Bass 20, Pluck 10, Acid 5, Atmos 5, FX 5, Pad 5, Reese 5 ([Loopmasters](https://www.loopmasters.com/genres/58-Psy-Trance/products/18716-Elements-Psytrance-Serum-Presets)); Serum 2 Psytrance, 155 presets ([Loopmasters](https://www.loopmasters.com/genres/58-Psy-Trance/products/19692-Serum-2-Psytrance)). Leads dominate psytrance libraries.

## 2. Rules used in AudioLab (decisions)

1. Every synth: "Init" first, then groups from one vocabulary: Lead, Bass, Sub, Pad, Strings, Pluck, Arp, Keys, Organ, Bells, Stab or Chord, FX, Psytrance (Acid gets Classic, Techno, Psytrance, Bass, Lead, FX; Growl keeps Dubstep, EDM, Drum and bass, Psytrance).
2. Names say what the sound is, in plain words; the group is not repeated in the name.
3. Every preset has a one-line tooltip in the preset menu: what it is for and where on the keyboard it sounds best (for example "C1 to C2"). Presets made for psytrance outside the Psytrance group are marked "· psy" and their tooltip ends "Made for psytrance."
4. Count per instrument: what the engine can make clearly different (measured, see 5). Simple instruments with 8 knobs: 11 to 20; Plant 68, Venom 72, Wavetable 48, Growl 36, Acid 31, Vector FM 27, Orbit 25.
5. Level: every preset is measured and trimmed to the instrument's default preset (pads 3 dB lower, keys 1.5 dB, plucks 1 dB); presets that demos use keep their exact values.
6. Effects inside presets stay light (Plant's Space, Venom's chorus): the user adds delay and reverb on the track.

## 3. Which real synth each AudioLab synth is closest to

| AudioLab | Closest real synth | What that means for presets |
|---|---|---|
| Saw lead, Square lead, Soft pad, Strings, Pluck, Soft keys, Sub bass, Square bass, Reese bass | a one-oscillator-type analog synth (two detuned oscillators, 12 dB low-pass with its own short envelope, ADSR): like a simple SH-101 or one layer of a Juno | the sound comes from wave, detune, cutoff, resonance and envelope only, so 12 to 20 presets each before they start to sound alike |
| Plant | Sylenth1 or Vital with fewer knobs: two waves with morph, up to 7 unison voices, filter with its own envelope (Bloom), FM, sub, noise, LFO to cutoff, Space reverb | supersaws, plucks, pads, keys, basses and FX all possible |
| Wavetable | Serum or Vital oscillator: 12 tables of 8 frames, position, scan LFO, position envelope, unison 5 | scanning pads, growls, zaps from a falling position envelope |
| Venom | Serum: two wavetable oscillators with warp, sub, noise, ladder, state-variable, comb and formant filters, 8-slot mod matrix, 4 macros, distortion, chorus | everything; the only synth here with macros (Macro 1 = cutoff, 2 = wave position, 3 = detune in every preset) |
| Acid | Roland TB-303: one oscillator, resonant filter, accent, slide, drive | acid lines from classic to psytrance |
| Growl | a growl patch in Serum or Phase Plant (FM pair, formant filter moved by a tempo LFO, OTT) | dubstep, EDM, DnB and talking psy mids |
| Vector FM, Orbit | FM pairs like a DX7 or Ableton Operator, moved by particles or orbits | bells, electric pianos, FM basses, moving pads |

## 4. Recipes used (numbers from sources; inferences marked)

- **Supersaw (Roland JP-8000)**: 7 saws, outer offsets about plus and minus 11 % of the detune setting, mix 0.75 ([analysis gist](https://gist.github.com/audionerd/fe50790b7601cba65ddd855caffb05ad), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supersaw)). Trance lead (Syntorial, Mark Sherry recipe): attack 12 ms, decay 305 ms, sustain 93 %, release 90 ms ([Syntorial](https://www.syntorial.com/preset-recipe/mark-sherry-imbecile-lead/)). Supersaw pad (Syntorial, Aura pad): attack 219 ms, decay 711 ms, sustain 90 %, release 372 ms ([Syntorial](https://www.syntorial.com/preset-recipe/jesse-maas-aura-pad/)).
- **Plucks**: trance pluck (Syntorial, Sash): filter envelope decay 250 ms, amp decay 550 ms, sustain 0, resonance 45 %, triangle with FM and a sub ([Syntorial](https://www.syntorial.com/preset-recipe/sash-encore-un-fois-pluck/)); Goa pluck (Entheogenic): square, low cutoff, 36 ms envelope, 37 ms release ([Syntorial](https://www.syntorial.com/preset-recipe/entheogenic-invisible-landscapes-pluck/)); house chord pluck (EMBRZ): 8 voices at small detune, filter decay 215 ms, release 14 ms ([Syntorial](https://www.syntorial.com/preset-recipe/embrz-higher-chords/)); more than about 30 cents of detune sounds out of tune ([Cymatics](https://cymatics.fm/blogs/production/creating-plucks-xfer-serum)).
- **Reese**: two saws plus and minus 55 cents, low-pass near 4 kHz ([MusicRadar](https://www.musicradar.com/how-to/classic-reese-bass-renegade-terrorist)); old-school: 50 cents, sustain 85 %, release 100 ms ([MusicRadar](https://www.musicradar.com/how-to/how-to-create-a-classic-old-school-reese-bass-sound)). The basic Reese bass allows at most 40 cents.
- **Acid**: Phuture "Acid Tracks": square, resonance 95 %, envelope decay 450 ms, release 20 ms ([Syntorial](https://www.syntorial.com/preset-recipe/phuture-acid-tracks-bass/)); 18 dB filter, resonance two-thirds or more ([MusicRadar](https://www.musicradar.com/news/producers-guide-roland-tb-303)); Diva acid: glide about a third, drive 63 % after the filter ([Attack](https://www.attackmagazine.com/technique/synth-secrets/acid-synth-uhe-diva/)); slides on 4 to 5 of 16 steps, glide 40 to 80 ms (project doc).
- **Psytrance lead** (project doc `claude/psytrance-rules-in-numbers.md`, Myloops): 3 saws a few cents apart, 24 dB low-pass at 400 to 800 Hz, resonance a third to a half, filter envelope decay 150 to 300 ms with sustain 20 to 40 %, amp sustain 70 to 80 %, about 5 Hz vibrato; psy lead with band-pass near 1 kHz and distortion after the filter ([DanceMidiSamples](https://www.dancemidisamples.com/?p=148453)). Psy pad: saws +7 and -5 cents, low-pass 1.2 kHz, resonance 25 to 35 %, attack 200 to 500 ms (project doc). Rolling bass: see `claude/audiolab-psy-bass-research-and-device.md`.
- **Zaps**: no numbers published; method only (self-oscillating filter or a sine, fast pitch fall, sustain 0) ([Soundfly](https://flypaper.soundfly.com/produce/pew-pew-how-to-make-laser-sounds-with-a-synth/)). Inference used: two octaves down in about 150 ms.
- **FM**: Chowning 1973: bell 1 to 1.4 with the index decaying over a long time; brass 1 to 1 with the index following the loudness; clarinet 3 to 2; wood drum 16 to 11 ([paper](https://web.eecs.umich.edu/%7Efessler/course/100/misc/chowning-73-tso.pdf)). DX7 E.Piano 1: tine modulator at 14 times the note ([Yamaha](https://yamahasynth.com/learn/synth-programming/fm-101-part-five-expanding-your-sound/)). FM bass: 1 to 1 plus a short 7 to 1 click ([Syntorial](https://www.syntorial.com/tutorials/synth-quickie-fm-bass/)).
- **Wobble and hoover**: LFO in 1/8 triplets on the cutoff ([MusicRadar](https://www.musicradar.com/how-to/lfo-wobble-bass)); hoover: detuned pulse saws plus and minus 40 cents, one an octave down, pitch envelope ([MusicRadar](https://musicradar.com/how-to/how-to-create-a-classic-90s-hoover-sound)).
- **Organ, strings**: house organ (Show Me Love): triangle plus square, low-pass ([MusicRadar](https://www.musicradar.com/how-to/classic-house-organ-robin-s-show-me-love)); string machine: pulse, low-pass 6 kHz, high-pass 200 Hz ([MusicRadar](https://musicradar.com/how-to/synth-string-synth-choir)); medium strings: attack 25 ms, very long release ([Syntorial](https://www.syntorial.com/preset-recipe/anthony-rother-medium-strings/)).
- **Pads**: Juno pad: saw plus sub, slow pitch drift, chorus ([MusicRadar](https://www.musicradar.com/how-to/how-to-create-an-80s-juno-style-pad-sound)); riser: pitch rising, long reverb ([Attack](https://attackmagazine.com/technique/tutorials/essential-transition-fx-technique-masterclass/2)).
- **Gaps (no published numbers)**: zap sweep times, growl wavetable positions, big room leads, impacts, sub drops. Those presets are my own choices.

## 5. How a preset is checked (for parts 2 and 3)

`tools/engine-tests/presets_all.py [index.html] [inst,inst]` renders every preset of an instrument with a phrase that fits its role (bass, sub, acid, lead, pluck, pad, keys, fx; the role comes from `PSMETA[inst][key].r` or the group), twice. It checks for NaN, silence, clipping (peak under 1), that the sound ends (last second 50 dB under the loudest part) and that two renders are the same; measures level (loudest 100 ms, K-weighted), attack (10 % to 50 %), spectral centre and the share under 200 Hz; and lists the closest pairs on a probe (C3 for 1 s, then a short C5; 24 bands by 12 time slices, mean difference in dB; under 1.5 dB counts as "nearly the same"). With `--json out.json` it writes the level trims; `PSLV` in index.html holds them. `tools/ui-tests/preset_menus.py` checks the menus (groups, Init first, a tooltip on every entry, choosing applies). How to add an instrument: give it a `PSMETA` entry per preset (group, tooltip, flags) in the PSLIB block of index.html and add its key to the lists in both tests.

## Part 2: drums, percussion, samplers, particle instruments, FX, Grid, Sub, Psy Bass, Bounce and Bouncy Notes (v0.202, 419 presets in 15 instruments and Bouncy Notes, 258 before)

### Research (sources read for this part)
- **Kicks, general numbers.** Kick fundamentals sit in the 30 to 70 Hz range, sweet spot 40 to 50 Hz (E0 41.2 Hz to A0 55 Hz in standard naming); 808 kick: sine, pitch decay 20 to 70 ms; 909 kick: triangle through a low-pass with a 200 to 500 ms pitch and filter decay ([MusicRadar, classic analogue drums](https://musicradar.com/how-to/how-to-recreate-classic-analogue-drum-sounds-in-your-daw-and-with-hardware)). Kick pitch sweep decay 20 to 80 ms (Vital example about 30 ms), body decay house 200 to 300 ms, techno 400 ms or more, click noise decay 5 to 15 ms ([Beat Kitchen](https://beatkitchen.io/guides/electronic-music/02-drum-design/)). Kick at about 58 Hz, pitch envelope attack about 1 ms and decay about 50 ms ([ModeAudio](https://modeaudio.com/magazine/drum-synth-sound-design-kick-snare)). Original 808 tuning 56 Hz ([MusicRadar, analogue-style kick](https://musicradar.com/how-to/make-analogue-style-kick)). House kick in Kick 2: length about 500 ms, drive about 60 % ([Computer Music via MusicRadar](https://musicradar.com/how-to/how-to-synthesise-a-house-kick-using-sonic-academy-kick-2)). Kick 2, BigKick and Punchbox are built the same way (a sine body with a pitch envelope, a click or sample layer, drive), which is what the Kick designer does; no factory preset values of them are published.
- **TR-808 bass drum circuit** (Werner, DAFx-14): trigger pulse 1 ms, an attack where the pitch is raised by more than an octave for about 6 ms, centre frequency about 49.5 Hz (Roland's chart: 56 Hz), a slow "pitch sigh" of about 48 to 58 Hz over the first 300 ms ([paper](https://dafx14.fau.de/papers/dafx14_kurt_james_werner_a_physically_informed,_ci.pdf)).
- **Psytrance kick** (project doc `claude/psytrance-rules-in-numbers.md`): sine with a fast fall plus a click high-passed at 1 kHz; shorter than 130 ms (Aciduss), full-on body 80 to 150 ms (Myloops), at most one 8th (Bonedo); fundamental E to A (41 to 55 Hz), tuned to the key; the rolling bass comes on the second 16th (103.4 ms at 145 BPM). The Fractals demo kick lasts to about 190 ms.
- **Hardstyle**: pitch from about C4 to C5 falling to F1 to C2 over 100 to 200 ms, a 5 to 15 ms "tok", heavy distortion, 150 to 160 BPM ([Music Production Wiki](https://musicproductionwiki.com/articles/how-to-recreate-the-hardstyle-kick), unsourced starting points; [EDMProd](https://www.edmprod.com/how-to-make-hardstyle/) gives no numbers).
- **808 bass**: Vital patch decay 1.44 s, sustain 55 %, release 0.25 s, pitch amount 15 to 25 % ([EDMProd](https://www.edmprod.com/808s/)).
- **Snares**: 808 snare a sine near 160 Hz, amp decay 250 to 350 ms, noise decay 100 to 600 ms; 909 snare pitch decay 100 to 200 ms; snare tone 100 Hz (beefy) to 200 Hz (thin) (MusicRadar above); tone 150 to 250 Hz with decay 80 to 150 ms and noise 100 to 250 ms (Beat Kitchen); about 200 Hz, short decay for tight genres (ModeAudio). Psytrance snare: three layers (909 punch, clap, stereo noise), no low end (Bonedo, in the project doc).
- **Claps**: 3 to 4 hits 10 to 20 ms apart, band-pass 1 to 3 kHz, tail 100 to 200 ms (Beat Kitchen); 808 clap band-pass 800 Hz to 1.5 kHz, amp decay 450 to 800 ms (MusicRadar).
- **Hats**: closed hat high-pass 5 to 8 kHz, decay 10 to 40 ms; open hat 200 to 500 ms; crash 1 to 3 s (Beat Kitchen); closed under 200 ms, open about 1 s (MusicRadar). The 808 metal: six square oscillators at 205.3, 304.4, 369.6, 522.7, 540 and 800 Hz, band-passes at about 3.44 and 7.1 kHz, high-pass near 10.5 kHz ([ICMC 2014 paper on the 808 cymbal](https://speech.di.uoa.gr/ICMC-SMC-2014/images/VOL_2/1453.pdf)); the Hat designer uses the same six frequencies. Psytrance closed hat must die within one 16th (project doc).
- **Toms**: 909 toms two oscillators a fifth apart, pitch envelope 100 to 200 ms; 808 cowbell 555 and 835 Hz, clave 1.5 to 3.5 kHz with 150 to 250 ms (MusicRadar).
- **Granular and sampler presets**: grains of 1 to 100 ms are typical; an ambient pad from about 600 ms grains every 40 ms with jitter, slow attack and release; LFOs under 0.1 Hz on size and interval for an evolving pad; small grains at low density for glitch ([Native Instruments](https://blog.native-instruments.com/granular-synthesis/)); large grains at high density blur into pads, small grains at low density give glitches; density as notes (1/16 gives a hat-like part) ([Sound On Sound](https://www.soundonsound.com/techniques/granular-synthesis-practical-introduction)); pads from long grains with spray and a slow scan, glitch with spray the length of the sample and random pitch ([EDMProd](https://www.edmprod.com/granular-synthesis/)). Simpler-style modes (Classic, One-shot, Slice) and warped playback ([Ableton help](https://help.ableton.com/hc/en-us/articles/209072629-Warping-in-Simpler)) are why the Sampler got settings-only presets that keep the user's own sample.
- **Risers, impacts, zaps**: from the project doc (noise riser band-pass 500 Hz to 8 kHz over 8 bars; downlifter: saw falling 2 octaves over a bar into a 4 to 6 s reverb; impact: sine falling about an octave plus body and crack; FX blip every 4 bars at -15 to -20 dB).
- **Sub and Psy Bass**: checked against their own research docs (`claude/audiolab-sub-bass-research-and-device.md`, `claude/audiolab-psy-bass-research-and-device.md`). Gaps filled: an Init in each; the "kick owns the sub" model (bass an octave above a kick on the root, high-passed at 80 to 90 Hz, Myloops in the project doc) as a Psy Bass preset; a twilight roller; a trance offbeat sub and a UK garage sliding sub.
- **Guesses (no published numbers)**: every value of the particle instruments (Emit, Vector Grain, Bounce, Bouncy Notes), the kit levels per pad, the FX variants' curves, the level trims between groups, and the genre kick values outside psytrance where only ranges exist.

### Rules added in part 2
1. Drums: the designers make every hit at the same peak, so their presets keep the designer's level; a kick preset never has a level of its own.
2. A key that a project or a drum pad can store by name (Kick designer presets, kit kicks) keeps its values for ever; a near copy is hidden from the menu (PSMETA h) rather than deleted.
3. Kits keep one pad layout (kick C3, rim, snare, clap, second snare, low tom, closed hat, mid tom, pedal hat, high tom, open hat, conga, shaker, crash, cowbell, ride), so beats move between kits.
4. Samplers: a group of settings that keeps the loaded sample, next to presets on the built-in sources.
5. Psytrance kicks for a rolling bass: 20 dB down at 103.4 ms (the first bass note at 145 BPM), ending on the key's root between E and A (41 to 55 Hz).
6. Tests for part 2: `presets_all.py index.html part2` (kick pitch and length table, the K-B-B-B roll for Psy Bass, phrase-based comparisons for rhythmic instruments), `bnotes_presets.py`, `preset_menus2.py`.

## Part 3: every effect and MIDI effect (v0.217, 957 presets in 40 effects, the Pro EQ and Chord; 843 before)

### Research (sources read for this part)
Numbers marked "general knowledge" are widely published starting points that I did not find on a page read in this run; "guess" means my own choice.

- **Compression per source.** Sound On Sound (drum compression): snare 5:1, fast attack, medium release, 4 to 5 dB of gain reduction; close mics 3 to 4 dB each, then smaller amounts on the drum bus and "a touch more" on the mix bus; kick outside mic 6 dB or more with fast attack and medium release; a room mic 8:1 with fast attack and release, driven ([SOS](https://www.soundonsound.com/techniques/drum-compression)). Bass: 3:1 to 4:1, attack about 100 ms backed off until the transient survives, release 20 to 40 ms, 5 to 8 dB of reduction; serial stages of 2 to 3 dB at 2:1 to 3:1 ([EDMProd](https://www.edmprod.com/compress-bass/)). Kick and bass group in psytrance: 1.5:1, 30 ms attack, 1 to 2 dB (project doc, Myloops). Bus glue (general knowledge, SSL-style bus compressor): 2:1 or 4:1, attack 10 or 30 ms, release 0.1 s or auto, 1 to 3 dB. Vocal: fast 1176-style 4:1 to 12:1 with 3 to 6 dB, or optical 3:1 with slow release (general knowledge).
- **Sidechain pumping.** Reason compressor at 123 BPM: threshold -36 dB and the fastest attack for full pumping, release about 39 ms, threshold -14 dB for a subtle bounce ([MusicRadar](https://www.musicradar.com/tuition/tech/how-to-create-classic-pumping-sidechain-compression-624915)); "a few dB" of bass ducking with fast attack and medium-fast release ([Attack Magazine](https://attackmagazine.com/technique/tutorials/ten-tips-better-drums/3)). Psytrance (project doc, Myloops): light 2 to 4 dB back to 0 in 40 to 70 ms; medium 4:1 to 8:1, 6 to 10 dB, release 80 to 120 ms; drawn volume shape silent on the beat, hold 30 to 50 ms, back over 60 to 80 ms; the whole duck must end inside one 16th (103.4 ms at 145 BPM).
- **Multiband.** Crossovers for a full mix 120 Hz and at least 2.5 kHz, heavy top work above 6 kHz; bass guitar 50 to 60 Hz and a few hundred Hz; voice 150 Hz and 4 kHz; a mastering mid band 1.1:1 at -35 dB; bass release about twice the mid release and the top band half of it ([SOS](https://www.soundonsound.com/techniques/multi-band-compression-tips)). Glue start for all bands 2:1 with 100 ms attack and release, crossovers 200 Hz and 2 kHz ([iZotope](https://www.izotope.com/en/learn/multiband-compression-basics-izotope-mastering-tips.html)). OTT (general knowledge): Xfer's free OTT is Ableton's Multiband Dynamics "OTT" preset, crossovers 88 Hz and 2.5 kHz, depth usually 20 to 50 % on a mix and up to 100 % on sound design.
- **Limiting and clipping for club masters.** A limiter usually does no more than about 2 dB, ceiling -1.0 to -0.1 dBFS; heavy settings (3 to 5 dB) audibly distort vocals; a loud dance master example ends near -8 LUFS ([iZotope limiters](https://www.izotope.com/en/learn/an-introduction-to-limiters-and-how-to-use-them)). A soft clipper before the limiter takes peaks first and can give up to about 6 dB ([iZotope soft clipping](https://www.izotope.com/en/learn/what-is-soft-clipping)); 2 dB of clipping plus 2 dB of gain sounds cleaner than 4 dB of clipping ([Slate Bus Clipper manual](https://docs.slatedigital.com/Bus_Clipper/Bus_Clipper.html)); clip after EQ and before the limiter, high-pass or leave the bass out of a clipper ([Mastering The Mix](https://masteringthemix.com/blogs/learn/how-to-use-a-clipper-when-mastering)). Psytrance master (project doc): mix peaks -6 dBFS into the limiter, at most 4 dB of limiting, ceiling -1 dBTP, about -8 LUFS integrated, -6.5 LUFS short-term in the drop; Electric Universe albums measure DR5 to DR3.
- **EQ per source.** Kick 60 to 80 Hz punch, 200 to 500 Hz box cut, 1 to 5 kHz beater; snare 150 to 250 Hz body, 400 to 700 Hz ring, 2 to 3.5 kHz attack; hats 3 to 7 kHz harshness; bass 300 to 500 Hz mud, 800 Hz to 1 kHz attack; vocal high-pass 100 to 250 Hz, 500 Hz to 1.2 kHz nasal, 5 to 8 kHz harsh, 10 kHz air shelf ([iZotope EQ cheat sheet](https://www.izotope.com/en/learn/eq-cheat-sheet)); kick 50 to 60 Hz, 400 Hz hollow, 3 to 5 kHz; bass 50 to 80, 700 Hz, 2.5 kHz; vocal 120, 240, 5 kHz presence, 10 to 15 kHz air (Owsinski via [Produce Like A Pro](https://producelikeapro.com/blog/eq-cheat-sheet/)). Psytrance (project doc): high-pass pads and FX at 300 Hz, reverb returns at 500 Hz, everything except kick and bass at 120 Hz at least; leads lose their side signal under 300 Hz; mono under 120 to 150 Hz.
- **Reverb.** Cut the low end of a reverb from 100 to 200 Hz down, tame metallic highs from 4 to 8 kHz, a little pre-delay keeps the dry sound in front ([MusicRadar](https://www.musicradar.com/how-to/explainers-reverb-parameters-and-how-they-work)). Gated reverb: hold about 70 ms, release about 140 ms ([SOS gates](https://www.soundonsound.com/techniques/production-techniques-using-gates-expanders)). Psytrance atmospheres: pads with heavy reverb in the background; long tails printed and cut ([Bonedo](https://www.bonedo.de/artikel/psytrance-produzieren-3-psy-fx-atmosphaeren/)). Pre-delay and decay against the tempo (general knowledge): pre-delay a 1/64 or 1/32 note (26 or 52 ms at 145 BPM), decay that ends before the next beat or bar (one bar is 1.66 s at 145 BPM).
- **Delay.** Psytrance FX and leads: long ping-pong delays ([Bonedo](https://www.bonedo.de/artikel/psytrance-produzieren-3-psy-fx-atmosphaeren/)); lead delay at a low mix ([IDM Mag](https://idmmag.com/tech/tutorials/waio-style-psytrance-leads-transitions/)); a tape delay at 1/16 on percussion and a reverb after a delay at about 35 % mix on an arp ([Attack Magazine](https://www.attackmagazine.com/technique/tutorials/adding-atmosphere-with-delay-and-reverb/)). Dotted eighths (0.75 beat, 310 ms at 145 BPM) and 3/16 echoes are the trance and psytrance default; slapback 70 to 120 ms; doubling 10 to 30 ms (general knowledge).
- **De-essing.** Sibilance 2 to 12 kHz, most offensive 5 to 8 kHz; 3 to 4 dB of reduction normal, up to about 10 dB for strong esses ([Splice](https://splice.com/blog/?p=23640)).
- **Gates.** Drum gates: attack from zero up until the click goes, release for a natural fade, hold allows short releases ([SOS gates](https://www.soundonsound.com/techniques/production-techniques-using-gates-expanders)). Psytrance bass gate: attack 0.01 to 0.1 ms, hold 100 to 130 ms, release 15 to 40 ms, at least -40 dB (project doc; treated as suspect because it mutes the first bass note).
- **Lo-fi.** E-mu SP-1200: 12-bit linear at 26.04 kHz ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/E-mu_SP-1200)), so a rate divider of about 1.7 at 44.1 kHz.
- **Chorus.** Roland Juno-60 chorus: two buttons with modulation rates of 0.4 Hz and 0.6 Hz, both together a stronger chorus ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Juno_60)).
- **Guesses (no published numbers)**: the particle and drawn effects (Vector Delay, Bounce Delay, Fractal, Blur, LFO Shaper curves), Frequency Shifter amounts, vocoder band counts per style, pitch-correction speeds outside the classic hard-tune, every tooltip's "best on" advice, and the level targets between groups.

### Rules added in part 3
1. Every effect: groups by use (source, task or character) and a "Psytrance" group; a one-line tooltip per preset says what it is for, and the menu adds the main settings in brackets (for example "4:1 from -18 dB, attack 30 ms, release 120 ms"). Presets made for psytrance outside the Psytrance group show "· psy".
2. Effects that started with no preset of their own (Compressor, Echo delay, Space reverb, Drive, Low-pass filter, Phaser) get "Init: Default settings" first, equal to a new effect. For every other effect the first preset, which a new effect starts on, keeps its sound, except the Timeless delay (see 5) and two levels that were clearly wrong (Multiband "Master glue" was 9.7 LU louder than bypass, the Vocoder "Classic robot" 13 LU quieter).
3. Every preset is filled with the settings of a new effect for the keys it does not set, so it sounds the same whichever preset was chosen before; choosing a preset copies its values (the effect no longer shares drawn shapes and arrays with the preset table).
4. Level: where an effect has an Output, presets are trimmed so the loudness against the dry sound stays within 1 LU (the Limiter and the Soft Clipper's mastering presets are louder on purpose). The Compressor adds its own make-up gain and Make-up cannot go below 0, so its presets are matched to each other at about +4 LU. Effects without an Output (Drive, Sidechain punch and sustain) keep their own levels.
5. Feedback: the Timeless delay's drive stage raises the loop gain to k/tanh(k) with k = 1 + drive x 0.06, and each filter adds about 1 dB, so its feedback is set from the drive for a chosen loop gain (0.45 to 0.78, each repeat 2 to 7 dB down). Its old default "Warm tape" (key tape) and "Ping-pong 1/8" (ping) build up for ever; the demos use them, so they stay, in a group "Endless (repeats build up)", and a new Timeless delay starts on a stable "Warm tape". The Echo delay is linear: at most 80 % feedback (90 % ran away and clipped 22 times over).
6. Old keys stay with their old values unless a value was wrong; renamed where a name had an artist, a brand or a product in it ("Dotted 1/8 (The Edge)", "Hard-tune (T-Pain)", "Kick tight 1176", "SP-1200 style", "Daft chord", "Kraftwerk", "Juno-style", "Dimension D", "NES", "Game Boy"), and where it promised something the effect cannot do (Compressor "Parallel crush" has no mix knob: now "Drums crushed, for a send"; reverb "Infinite freeze" stops at 8 s).
7. Tests for part 3: `tools/engine-tests/fx_materials.py` (test material), `fx_presets.py` (every preset), `fx_trims.py` (level trims), `fx_demo_same.py` (demos and new-effect settings), `tools/ui-tests/preset_menus3.py` (menus).
