# AudioLab preset library: research and how the presets are built (part 1, the synths)

Written for parts 2 (the other instruments) and 3 (the effects) of the preset task, and for anyone tuning presets later. The release is v0.197 (451 presets in 16 instruments, 229 before). Nobody has listened to these presets: they come from published recipes, then measurement.

## 1. What makes a good preset (sources)

- **Playable straight away, shows what the synth can do.** Urs Heckmann (u-he): good presets let people "make music right away"; Reveal Sound: presets "should reveal the potential of the synthesiser". Both say quality matters more than quantity. [MusicRadar roundup of developers](https://musicradar.com/news/tech/must-a-synth-come-with-a-large-supply-of-good-presets-639042)
- **Curate.** Serum 1 shipped about 450 presets cut down from about 1,000 submitted (Steve Duda, same article). Serum 2: 626 presets ([Xfer](https://www.xferrecords.com/products/serum-2)). u-he counts: Diva 1354, Hive 2 2420, Zebra2 551 ([u-he](https://new.u-he.com/support/faq/product-info/preset-count)). Vital's own factory set is small (43 "Factory", 235 with the packs, [Vital forum](https://forum.vital.audio/t/number-of-presets-and-waveforms-in-vital-plus/5164)).
- **Bread and butter plus showcase.** Massive X designers aimed at usable "bread and butter" sounds and a few extreme ones. [NI blog](https://blog.native-instruments.com/massive-x-lab-sound-design/)
- **Not drowned in effects; fits a mix.** Factory presets "are designed to sell the synth" and often need stripping down ([Gearnews](https://www.gearnews.com/?p=259059), [MusicRadar](https://musicradar.com/how-to/how-to-strip-back-a-synth-preset-to-make-it-more-usable)); leave room in the top end, use filter envelopes sparingly ([MusicRadar](https://musicradar.com/tuition/tech/10-ways-to-design-better-synth-patches-634900)).
- **Init preset first**: one saw, filter open, gate envelope, no effects, no glide ([Gearnews](https://www.gearnews.com/?p=259059)).
- **Naming** (Steinberg HALion preset guideline): English, no artist or song names, no leading numbers, no category word in the name when the browser already shows the category. [Steinberg](https://steinbergmedia.github.io/halion-script-api/HALion-Tutorials-Guidelines/pages/HS-Preset-Guideline.html)
- **Categories**: Vital uses Bass, Lead, Keys, Pad, Percussion, Sequence, Experiment, SFX, Template ([Vital forum](https://forum.vital.audio/t/categories-for-arps-brass-etc/6362)); NKS advice: a small fixed vocabulary, no synonyms ([NI community](https://community.native-instruments.com/discussion/comment/46421)).
- **Gain staging**: the only published numbers found are Steinberg's (single note at full velocity: basses and leads near -1 to 0 dB, polyphonic pads about -6 dB so chords fit). Users complain about hot or uneven presets ([KVR, Diva levels](https://www.kvraudio.com/forum/viewtopic.php?p=4900736); [KVR](https://www.kvraudio.com/forum/viewtopic.php?p=8009867)).
- **Performance controls**: Steinberg: always assign the mod wheel; u-he soundsets: every preset uses at least one performance control; vibrato is the usual mod wheel job, also filter or wavetable position ([u-he Automata readme](https://u-he.com/downloads/manuals/soundsets/diva/Automata_ReadMe.pdf), [MusicRadar](https://www.musicradar.com/how-to/how-to-design-an-expressive-synth-patch-for-performance)).
- **Psytrance packs**: Elements Psytrance (Serum), 100 presets: Leads 45, Bass 20, Pluck 10, Acid 5, Atmos 5, FX 5, Pad 5, Reese 5 ([Loopmasters](https://www.loopmasters.com/genres/58-Psy-Trance/products/18716-Elements-Psytrance-Serum-Presets)); Serum 2 Psytrance, 155 presets ([Loopmasters](https://www.loopmasters.com/genres/58-Psy-Trance/products/19692-Serum-2-Psytrance)). Leads dominate psytrance libraries.

## 2. Rules used in AudioLab (decisions)

1. Every synth: "Init" first, then groups from one vocabulary: Lead, Bass, Sub, Pad, Strings, Pluck, Arp, Keys, Organ, Bells, Stab or Chord, FX, Psytrance (Acid gets Classic, Techno, Psytrance, Bass, Lead, FX; Growl keeps Dubstep, EDM, Drum and bass, Psytrance).
2. Names say what the sound is, in plain words; the group is not repeated in the name.
3. Every preset has a one-line tooltip in the preset menu: what it is for and where on the keyboard it sounds best (for example "C1 to C2"). Presets made for psytrance outside the Psytrance group are marked "· psy" and their tooltip ends "Made for psytrance."
4. Count per instrument: what the engine can make clearly different (measured, see 5). Simple instruments with 8 knobs: 11 to 20; Plant 68, Venom 72, Wavetable 48, Growl 36, Acid 31, Vector FM 27, Orbit 25.
5. Level: every preset is measured and trimmed to the instrument's default preset (pads 3 dB lower, keys 1.5 dB, plucks 1 dB); presets that demos use keep their exact values.
6. Effects inside presets stay light (Plant's Space, Venom's chorus): the user adds delay and reverb on the track.

## 3. Which real synth each AudioLab synth is closest to

| AudioLab | Closest real synth | What that means for presets |
|---|---|---|
| Saw lead, Square lead, Soft pad, Strings, Pluck, Soft keys, Sub bass, Square bass, Reese bass | a one-oscillator-type analog synth (two detuned oscillators, 12 dB low-pass with its own short envelope, ADSR): like a simple SH-101 or one layer of a Juno | the sound comes from wave, detune, cutoff, resonance and envelope only, so 12 to 20 presets each before they start to sound alike |
| Plant | Sylenth1 or Vital with fewer knobs: two waves with morph, up to 7 unison voices, filter with its own envelope (Bloom), FM, sub, noise, LFO to cutoff, Space reverb | supersaws, plucks, pads, keys, basses and FX all possible |
| Wavetable | Serum or Vital oscillator: 12 tables of 8 frames, position, scan LFO, position envelope, unison 5 | scanning pads, growls, zaps from a falling position envelope |
| Venom | Serum: two wavetable oscillators with warp, sub, noise, ladder, state-variable, comb and formant filters, 8-slot mod matrix, 4 macros, distortion, chorus | everything; the only synth here with macros (Macro 1 = cutoff, 2 = wave position, 3 = detune in every preset) |
| Acid | Roland TB-303: one oscillator, resonant filter, accent, slide, drive | acid lines from classic to psytrance |
| Growl | a growl patch in Serum or Phase Plant (FM pair, formant filter moved by a tempo LFO, OTT) | dubstep, EDM, DnB and talking psy mids |
| Vector FM, Orbit | FM pairs like a DX7 or Ableton Operator, moved by particles or orbits | bells, electric pianos, FM basses, moving pads |

## 4. Recipes used (numbers from sources; inferences marked)

- **Supersaw (Roland JP-8000)**: 7 saws, outer offsets about plus and minus 11 % of the detune setting, mix 0.75 ([analysis gist](https://gist.github.com/audionerd/fe50790b7601cba65ddd855caffb05ad), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supersaw)). Trance lead (Syntorial, Mark Sherry recipe): attack 12 ms, decay 305 ms, sustain 93 %, release 90 ms ([Syntorial](https://www.syntorial.com/preset-recipe/mark-sherry-imbecile-lead/)). Supersaw pad (Syntorial, Aura pad): attack 219 ms, decay 711 ms, sustain 90 %, release 372 ms ([Syntorial](https://www.syntorial.com/preset-recipe/jesse-maas-aura-pad/)).
- **Plucks**: trance pluck (Syntorial, Sash): filter envelope decay 250 ms, amp decay 550 ms, sustain 0, resonance 45 %, triangle with FM and a sub ([Syntorial](https://www.syntorial.com/preset-recipe/sash-encore-un-fois-pluck/)); Goa pluck (Entheogenic): square, low cutoff, 36 ms envelope, 37 ms release ([Syntorial](https://www.syntorial.com/preset-recipe/entheogenic-invisible-landscapes-pluck/)); house chord pluck (EMBRZ): 8 voices at small detune, filter decay 215 ms, release 14 ms ([Syntorial](https://www.syntorial.com/preset-recipe/embrz-higher-chords/)); more than about 30 cents of detune sounds out of tune ([Cymatics](https://cymatics.fm/blogs/production/creating-plucks-xfer-serum)).
- **Reese**: two saws plus and minus 55 cents, low-pass near 4 kHz ([MusicRadar](https://www.musicradar.com/how-to/classic-reese-bass-renegade-terrorist)); old-school: 50 cents, sustain 85 %, release 100 ms ([MusicRadar](https://www.musicradar.com/how-to/how-to-create-a-classic-old-school-reese-bass-sound)). The basic Reese bass allows at most 40 cents.
- **Acid**: Phuture "Acid Tracks": square, resonance 95 %, envelope decay 450 ms, release 20 ms ([Syntorial](https://www.syntorial.com/preset-recipe/phuture-acid-tracks-bass/)); 18 dB filter, resonance two-thirds or more ([MusicRadar](https://www.musicradar.com/news/producers-guide-roland-tb-303)); Diva acid: glide about a third, drive 63 % after the filter ([Attack](https://www.attackmagazine.com/technique/synth-secrets/acid-synth-uhe-diva/)); slides on 4 to 5 of 16 steps, glide 40 to 80 ms (project doc).
- **Psytrance lead** (project doc `claude/psytrance-rules-in-numbers.md`, Myloops): 3 saws a few cents apart, 24 dB low-pass at 400 to 800 Hz, resonance a third to a half, filter envelope decay 150 to 300 ms with sustain 20 to 40 %, amp sustain 70 to 80 %, about 5 Hz vibrato; psy lead with band-pass near 1 kHz and distortion after the filter ([DanceMidiSamples](https://www.dancemidisamples.com/?p=148453)). Psy pad: saws +7 and -5 cents, low-pass 1.2 kHz, resonance 25 to 35 %, attack 200 to 500 ms (project doc). Rolling bass: see `claude/audiolab-psy-bass-research-and-device.md`.
- **Zaps**: no numbers published; method only (self-oscillating filter or a sine, fast pitch fall, sustain 0) ([Soundfly](https://flypaper.soundfly.com/produce/pew-pew-how-to-make-laser-sounds-with-a-synth/)). Inference used: two octaves down in about 150 ms.
- **FM**: Chowning 1973: bell 1 to 1.4 with the index decaying over a long time; brass 1 to 1 with the index following the loudness; clarinet 3 to 2; wood drum 16 to 11 ([paper](https://web.eecs.umich.edu/%7Efessler/course/100/misc/chowning-73-tso.pdf)). DX7 E.Piano 1: tine modulator at 14 times the note ([Yamaha](https://yamahasynth.com/learn/synth-programming/fm-101-part-five-expanding-your-sound/)). FM bass: 1 to 1 plus a short 7 to 1 click ([Syntorial](https://www.syntorial.com/tutorials/synth-quickie-fm-bass/)).
- **Wobble and hoover**: LFO in 1/8 triplets on the cutoff ([MusicRadar](https://www.musicradar.com/how-to/lfo-wobble-bass)); hoover: detuned pulse saws plus and minus 40 cents, one an octave down, pitch envelope ([MusicRadar](https://musicradar.com/how-to/how-to-create-a-classic-90s-hoover-sound)).
- **Organ, strings**: house organ (Show Me Love): triangle plus square, low-pass ([MusicRadar](https://www.musicradar.com/how-to/classic-house-organ-robin-s-show-me-love)); string machine: pulse, low-pass 6 kHz, high-pass 200 Hz ([MusicRadar](https://musicradar.com/how-to/synth-string-synth-choir)); medium strings: attack 25 ms, very long release ([Syntorial](https://www.syntorial.com/preset-recipe/anthony-rother-medium-strings/)).
- **Pads**: Juno pad: saw plus sub, slow pitch drift, chorus ([MusicRadar](https://www.musicradar.com/how-to/how-to-create-an-80s-juno-style-pad-sound)); riser: pitch rising, long reverb ([Attack](https://attackmagazine.com/technique/tutorials/essential-transition-fx-technique-masterclass/2)).
- **Gaps (no published numbers)**: zap sweep times, growl wavetable positions, big room leads, impacts, sub drops. Those presets are my own choices.

## 5. How a preset is checked (for parts 2 and 3)

`tools/engine-tests/presets_all.py [index.html] [inst,inst]` renders every preset of an instrument with a phrase that fits its role (bass, sub, acid, lead, pluck, pad, keys, fx; the role comes from `PSMETA[inst][key].r` or the group), twice. It checks for NaN, silence, clipping (peak under 1), that the sound ends (last second 50 dB under the loudest part) and that two renders are the same; measures level (loudest 100 ms, K-weighted), attack (10 % to 50 %), spectral centre and the share under 200 Hz; and lists the closest pairs on a probe (C3 for 1 s, then a short C5; 24 bands by 12 time slices, mean difference in dB; under 1.5 dB counts as "nearly the same"). With `--json out.json` it writes the level trims; `PSLV` in index.html holds them. `tools/ui-tests/preset_menus.py` checks the menus (groups, Init first, a tooltip on every entry, choosing applies). How to add an instrument: give it a `PSMETA` entry per preset (group, tooltip, flags) in the PSLIB block of index.html and add its key to the lists in both tests.
