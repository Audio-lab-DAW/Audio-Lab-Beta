# Backlog run log

Notes from the scheduled backlog runs. Newest at the bottom.

## 25 November 2026: v0.159, task 1 of 7 (mixer settings, master extras, fader designs)

Note: a manual run (2:54) and the scheduled 3:05 run overlapped; the 3:05 run was cancelled by the user and pushed nothing (checked: main was still at v0.158, no extra branches or tags).

### Built
- **Mixer settings button** `#mxSetB` in the mixer tab, right of `#mxCmp`. Opens the drop-down `#mxsPop` (`mxsPop()`).
- **Settings › Layout › Mixer** (`#mxsSet`, placed above Level meters). Same controls plus advanced ones: channel number, master strip width, fader height (left end = automatic), highest-peak read-out per channel, group colour band.
- Both places are built by `mxsHtml(adv)` and edit the same values, so they stay in sync (`mxsSync()`): `UI.mxs` (defaults in `MXSD`, read with `mxsO()`), `UI.mxCompact` (same value as the compact button) and `UI.mtr` (meter speed, reading, peak hold, colours, the same as Level meters). `mtrUI` and `syncSettings` are wrapped so changes made in one place show in the other.
- What strips show: effect slots, sends, picture, pan, mute/solo/record, dB scale, volume number. These are window classes on `#mixWin` (`mxsApply()`): `mxnofx`, `mxnosnd`, `mxnov`, `mxnopan`, `mxnobtn`, `mxnosc`, `mxnoval`, `mxnonum`, `mxshowpk`, `mxgb`. Strip widths are set through the style tag `#mxsCss`.
- **Master strip extras** (`.mxme`, built in `mxsMount()`, updated by `mxsMaster()` and `mxsTick()`):
  - True peak (`LM.tpMax`, turns amber/red against the target's peak limit; click to reset).
  - Loudness target (distance in LU from `lmTarget()`, with a bar and a green ±1 LU band; click to pick the target, which stays in sync with the loudness pop-up `#lmSel`).
  - Stereo correlation -1..+1 (computed from `anL`/`anR`, smoothed; red below 0).
  - Each one can be hidden in the mixer settings (`mLu`, `mTp`, `mTgt`, `mCorr`).
- **15 fader and meter designs** (`FDS`, CSS generated by `fdCssUp()` into `#fdCss`, scope class `fdN` on `#mixWin`). The designs are: 1 Classic (the old look, default), 2 Console, 3 LED ladder, 4 Dots, 5 Hairline, 6 Glass, 7 Flat bars, 8 Zones, 9 Level colour, 10 Neon, 11 Vintage, 12 In the groove, 13 Big blocks, 14 Rails, 15 Line and dot.
  - Each design is shown in both settings places as a small preview with its name.
  - The meters stay the existing `clip-path` bars with `<s>` peak lines, so instant levels, hold and the meter colour setting work in all 15.
  - Zones uses stepped colours from `lvStops()`. Level colour paints the whole bar in the colour of the current level (`mxsTick`).
- Fixes found while building:
  - The master's side meters never moved. `drawMixer` looks for `.fm` in the canvas's parent, and the master canvas sits in `.mxvw`. `mxsTick` now drives them.
  - With the picture hidden in the meter view, the side meters are driven too.
  - Channel names no longer shrink away in small windows (`.mx>.mxn{flex:none}`).
  - `mxsFit()` (wraps `mxFit`) applies the fixed fader height and makes faders shorter when the master would not fit. In a very small window it hides the master extras (`.mxtight`).

### Tested
- Headless Playwright with the demo projects (10 and 30 tracks), playing. No page errors on load or during the tests.
- All 15 designs switched live while playing and screenshotted on channels and the master.
- Checked:
  - every toggle and slider in the drop-down
  - sync with Settings and with the compact button
  - meter colour (Heat, Ice) carrying into the designs
  - peak hold from Settings
  - the target menu
  - saving across reload
  - fit at 470 px and 360 px window heights, compared with v0.158

### Not verified
- How it looks and how fast it runs on the user's Mac. CSS masks (designs 2, 3, 4, 11, 13, 15) on 30 strips at 120 Hz could cost frames.
- The true-peak read-out shows the maximum since the last reset (from the loudness worklet), not a live value.

## 25 November 2026: v0.160, task 2 of 7 (mixer view colours, spectrogram, fun views)

### Built
- **Bigger view drop-down** (`#ctxm.mxvm`): wider, larger text, bigger previews, descriptions wrap. New last entry **Colours and options** opens the colour and option menu of the chosen view (`mxMenuItems`, now reassigned to handle every view). Right-clicking a picture still opens the same menu.
- **Colour schemes for every view** (`MXMODES`, `MXPAL`, tables from `mxLut()`): temperature heat, three bands (bass red, mids green, treble blue, like Serato/Traktor), bass heat (hot when the bass is loud), fire, ice, purple spectral, mono white, track colour; meters keep green/yellow/red zones, spectrum keeps rainbow. Saved per view in `UI.mxCol` as before.
  - Band levels per channel: `mxBands()` (5 bands in `H0.b5`, 3-band bytes in `H0.b3`; waveform rows keep their band colour in `H0.bc`). Only computed when a band scheme or the Fun view is used.
  - Colour helpers: `mxC()`, `mxTriRGB()` (cached), `mxPosRGB()` (colour by position along the frequency axis).
- **Stereo field size**: Small, Medium, Large (`mxOpt('stereo','size')`, default Large = old look).
- **Spectrogram view** (`UI.mxView='sgram'`, `mxSgram()`): 64 x 160 ring-buffer canvas per channel (`H0.sg`), bass left, treble right, newest at the top, scaled into the strip. Default colours purple spectral; all schemes available.
- **Fun view** (`UI.mxView='fun'`, `mxFun()`, choice in `mxOpt('fun','anim')`, list `MXFUN`): fireflies (drift with stereo position, more of them light up and glow brighter with level), liquid surface (spring-driven slosh from the balance, rises with level, ripples on hits, dB ticks at the side), kaleidoscope (waveform mirrored 8 times, bigger when louder, turns with the balance), bouncing balls (5 bands, bass left, launch speed from band level, peak marks), particle fountain (gravity, wall and floor bounces, particle-to-particle collisions, max 70 per strip, hits throw bursts, dashed line at recent height). Default colours "AudioLab blue and teal" (the logo's ball colours, cached ball/glow sprites in `mxSpr()`).
- Channels scrolled out of view are still skipped in `drawMixer()`.

### Tested
- Headless Playwright, demo project (30 tracks) playing at bar 48: no page errors on load or while switching every view and several schemes; screenshots of each view, the master strip and both menus checked.
- Time per `drawMixer()` with 17 visible strips (headless, software drawing): 3 to 5 ms for the new views and schemes, same range as the old waveform view (stereo field is still the slowest at about 9 ms).
- Choosing a view and an animation from the menus saves to `audiolab-ui`.

### Not verified
- Look and frame rate on the user's Mac. In headless runs the spectrogram fills slowly because the test browser draws few frames; at 60 updates a second it covers about 2.7 s.
- Track analysers use 256-point FFTs, so the bass part of track spectrograms and the lowest ball are coarse (the master uses 2048).

## 25 November 2026: v0.161, task 3 of 7 (spectrum overlay fixes, dockable analyser with modules)

### Research (how analyser suites lay this out)
- iZotope Insight 2: one window made of modules (loudness, levels, sound field, spectrogram, spectrum, history) that are switched on and off with buttons in the title bar and share the space; the sound field puts the correlation bar beside or under the goniometer.
- Cubase: the right zone / meter bridge is a narrow strip with a Loudness tab that lists momentary, short-term, integrated, range and true peak as numbers beside a bar with the target marked.
- Followed here: module buttons in the title bar, numbers beside loudness bars with a green target band, goniometer with L and R on the diagonals and mono upright, correlation -1 to +1 with red below zero, spectrogram with bass at the bottom and newest on the right.

### Built (all new code sits before `askChoice`, names start with `anz` / `ANZ`; the old `spo*` functions are reassigned)
- **Steady spectrum scale** (`ANZD.spec`): the top of the picture is fixed at -24 dB (`ANZTOP`, measured so a very loud master sits about 12 dB under the top). Height can be 60, 72 (default) or 96 dB, or "Slow automatic", which only moves after the song has been louder or quieter for several seconds, in 6 dB steps. The old code rescaled on every frame.
- **Rising line when stopped: fixed.** Cause: silent bins (minus infinity) were turned into -120 dB, the treble tilt of 4.5 dB per octave was added, and the auto scale then zoomed in on that tilted line. Silent bins now stay on the floor and nothing is drawn without sound.
- **Glass border**: the window is now `.spov.anz` with a separate glass layer `.spgl` (class `lg` for the edge light, `rfAttach` for refraction) behind it. The canvases live in `.anzb`, a layer of their own above the glass, never inside it. Docked strips are solid (no glass at all).
- **Docking** (`anzSetDock`, `anzDock`, `anzSide`; button in the title bar or right-click): window, or a strip at the bottom (`#botOut`), top (`#topOut`), left or right (fixed, `#stage` gets a margin through `body.anz-l` / `body.anz-r` and `--anzl` / `--anzr`). Strips are resized by dragging their inner edge (`.anzrs`; `ov.dh`, `ov.dw`). One strip per side; `applyLayout` is wrapped so strips stay next to the main window. Docked analysers are saved and come back (`spoSave`, `UI.spov` now also holds `dock, mods, col, rng, dh, dw`).
- **Modules** (`ANZM`, drawn by `ANZD`, built by `anzBuild`): spectrum, spectrogram, stereo field, scrolling waveform, loudness (momentary, short-term and integrated bars, true peak max, range, target band), correlation (with a mark at the lowest recent value). Switch them with the buttons in the title bar, drag those buttons or the module labels to reorder (`anzWire`), or right-click for a menu (`anzMenu`). In a row the stereo field is square, loudness and correlation are narrow; in a column (left, right, or a tall window) they stack. The same modules work in the floating window. The first time an analyser is docked all modules are switched on.
- **Colours** (`anzCols` reads the schemes from `MXMODES`, so schemes added to the mixer views later appear here too): source colour, heat, three bands, bass heat, rainbow, fire, ice, purple spectral, AudioLab blue and teal, mono white. Uses `mxLut`, `mxC`, `mxPosRGB`, `mxBands`.
- **Idle**: after about 2 seconds without sound the pictures are only checked 3 times a second (`anzFrame`).
- Comparing several sources still works in the spectrum; the other modules show the first source. Loudness is always the master.

### Tested
- Headless Playwright, demo project (30 tracks) playing at bar 48: no page errors on load or during the tests.
- Screenshots checked: floating window with one and with six modules, tall window (column), docked bottom, top, left and right with different colour schemes, the right-click menu, and the picture 0.6 s and 4 s after stop (curve falls away, no rising line).
- Stage size checked for each dock (for example left: strip 250 px, stage starts at 250). Reorder by dragging a title bar button, toggle by click, two sources, save and reload (strip came back docked with the same modules, order and colours), close (body classes and `UI.spov` cleared).
- One frame with all six modules took about 1.7 ms (headless, software drawing).

### Not verified
- Look, refraction edge and frame rate on the user's Mac.
- Dragging a strip's inner edge to resize it and dragging module labels (only the title bar buttons were dragged in the test).
- The research came from general knowledge of these products; the web search only returned page links, not their text.
- Track sources use the track's 256-sample analysers for stereo field, waveform and correlation, so those are coarser than for the master.
- The older "Visualizer" panel (Settings) still exists next to this; both can be docked left or right together but that combination was not tested.
