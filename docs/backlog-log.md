### Built
- **Mixer settings button** `#mxSetB` in the mixer tab, right of `#mxCmp`. Opens the drop-down `#mxsPop` (`mxsPop()`).
- **Settings › Layout › Mixer** (`#mxsSet`, placed above Level meters). Same controls plus advanced ones: channel number, master strip width, fader height (left end = automatic), highest-peak read-out per channel, group colour band.
- Both places are built by `mxsHtml(adv)` and edit the same values, so they stay in sync (`mxsSync()`): `UI.mxs` (defaults in `MXSD`, read with `mxsO()`), `UI.mxCompact` (same value as the compact button) and `UI.mtr` (meter speed, reading, peak hold, colours, the same as Level meters). `mtrUI` and `syncSettings` are wrapped so changes made in one place show in the other.
- What strips show: effect slots, sends, picture, pan, mute/solo/record, dB scale, volume number. These are window classes on `#mixWin` (`mxsApply()`): `mxnofx`, `mxnosnd`, `mxnov`, `mxnopan`, `mxnobtn`, `mxnosc`, `mxnoval`, `mxnonum`, `mxshowpk`, `mxgb`. Strip widths are set through the style tag `#mxsCss`.
- **Master strip extras** (`.mxme`, built in `mxsMount()`, updated by `mxsMaster()` and `mxsTick()`):
  - True peak (`LM.tpMax`, turns amber/red against the target's peak limit; click to reset).
  - Loudness target (distance in LU from `lmTarget()`, with a bar and a green ±1 LU band; click to pick the target, which stays in sync with the loudness pop-up `#lmSel`).
  - Stereo correlation -1..+1 (computed from `anL`/`anR`, smoothed; red below 0).
  - Each one can be hidden in the mixer settings (`mLu`, `mTp`, `mTgt`, `mCorr`).
- **15 fader and meter designs** (`FDS`, CSS generated by `fdCssUp()` into `#fdCss`, scope class `fdN` on `#mixWin`). The designs are: 1 Classic (the old look, default), 2 Console, 3 LED ladder, 4 Dots, 5 Hairline, 6 Glass, 7 Flat bars, 8 Zones, 9 Level colour, 10 Neon, 11 Vintage, 12 In the groove, 13 Big blocks, 14 Rails, 15 Line and dot.
  - Each design is shown in both settings places as a small preview with its name.
  - The meters stay the existing `clip-path` bars with `<s>` peak lines, so instant levels, hold and the meter colour setting work in all 15.
  - Zones uses stepped colours from `lvStops()`. Level colour paints the whole bar in the colour of the current level (`mxsTick`).
- Fixes found while building:
  - The master's side meters never moved. `drawMixer` looks for `.fm` in the canvas's parent, and the master canvas sits in `.mxvw`. `mxsTick` now drives them.
  - With the picture hidden in the meter view, the side meters are driven too.
  - Channel names no longer shrink away in small windows (`.mx>.mxn{flex:none}`).
  - `mxsFit()` (wraps `mxFit`) applies the fixed fader height and makes faders shorter when the master would not fit. In a very small window it hides the master extras (`.mxtight`).
