| S1 | Dipole field | Field lines of a magnet (r = L sin²θ), as around Jupiter, tilted 10 degrees, charges sliding along them. The logo sits where the planet would be. |
| S2 | Moons in resonance | Io, Europa and Ganymede: 4, 2 and 1 orbits in the same time (the Laplace resonance), which is an octave and two octaves. |

### The terms, as read

- **NASA** (https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/): NASA content is "generally not subject to copyright in the United States"; "NASA should be acknowledged as the source"; content "used in a factual manner that does not imply endorsement may be used without needing explicit permission". For commercial use it "must not explicitly or implicitly convey NASA's endorsement of commercial goods or services", and clearances may be needed for pictures with NASA logos or NASA employees used as cover art or in promotional content (none of ours has either). The NASA insignia and logotype are protected and must not be used. Third-party material is marked with the copyright holder's name on the page; none of the five NASA pages had such a mark.
- **ESA/Webb** (https://esawebb.org/copyright/) and **ESA/Hubble** (https://esahubble.org/copyright/): images are released under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International and "may on a non-exclusive basis be reproduced without fee provided they are clearly and visibly credited"; the full credit line of each image is required; the material may not be used to state or imply endorsement by ESA/Webb; their logos are not covered.
- Not read: the JPL Image Use Policy page (https://www.jpl.nasa.gov/jpl-image-use-policy/) refused the request from the cloud machine. The NASA guidelines above were read in full instead; open the JPL page once by hand before shipping a JPL picture.

### Rules for the app if a picture is used

1. Show the full credit line on the same screen as the picture (small mono text in a corner of the splash; a line in About), and list all credits in About.
2. No wording such as "NASA", "JPL" or "Webb" in the app's name, marketing or store text that could read as endorsement; no agency logos anywhere.
3. Ship a downscaled copy inside the app (no loading from the internet at start); keep the original id and page in a comment.
4. CC BY 4.0 pictures: if a picture is cropped, darkened or has a drawing on top, say so in the About credits ("cropped and darkened").
5. Considered and left out: Juno pictures processed by citizen scientists (the processing has its own licence holders), Webb's Jupiter (processing credited to an outside person), the 1998 Hubble aurora plate PIA01254 (text burned into the picture), the Juno magnetic-field maps PIA25035 and PIA25040 (white-background charts).

## How the drawings are kept light

One small canvas per option; one shared timer at 30 frames a second; a drawing runs only while it is on screen (IntersectionObserver) and the page is visible; device pixel ratio capped at 2; no blur, no shadows on the canvas. The two picture-like options work on tiny buffers scaled up (interference 96 x 60, spectrogram 200 x 64, one new column per frame). With "reduce motion" on, every drawing stands still on a finished frame. In the app a splash drawing lives only while the splash is up; an About drawing must stop with the window and follow the app's freeze-in-background state. They can later run on the glass engine's canvas (plan 2.8).
