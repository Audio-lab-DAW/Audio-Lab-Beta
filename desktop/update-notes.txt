The mixer channels are reworked: pan can be a slider, a half dial or a full dial, and each one shows a small marker where the sound really sits (green when it is in the centre); the width slider shows the live stereo field, and the new Flat bars 2 faders and meters are the default. Double-click a track or channel to open its devices, double right-click to open the mixer on it; the master can be pinned, compact channels also make the mixer shorter, and the Mixer button glows while the mixer is open.
