New instrument Bounce: every note drops a ball, and every landing strikes wood, glass, metal, a string or a drum, louder and brighter the harder it hits, free or bouncing in time with the song. Bouncy Notes gets chord steps that change through the bar and a rhythm per ball; in Orbit you can throw a ball by dragging, remove one with a right-click, change its mass by scrolling over it, and see each ball's orbit.
