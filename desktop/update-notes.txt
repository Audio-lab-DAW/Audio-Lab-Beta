New device Fractal, as an instrument and as an effect: a row of short pulses with one level dial per step chops a sound, from a sidechain pump or trance gate in time with the song up to a rattle, growl, buzz or crackle at audio speed, in tune with the key if you want. It has its own phaser and 3D spread, one click adds Phaser or Vector Delay after it, and it comes with 20 presets.
