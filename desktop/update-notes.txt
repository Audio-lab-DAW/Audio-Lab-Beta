MIDI controllers, step 3: Ableton Push 1 support. Plug in your Push 1: its display shows the track, key and octave, the pads play the selected track in the project key (in key or chromatic, fourths, thirds or in a row), coloured from the track, and Scales on the Push changes key, layout, velocity, pressure and the touch strip.
Behind the scenes for the kick and bass tuning lab: AudioLab can now read which note your kick settles on (exactly for the Kick designer and drum machine kicks, measured for kick samples) and how each bass note of a bar sits against it, in tune or beating. The view that shows it comes next.
Settings, step 3: the Mono and L↔R buttons on the master strip now light up when you change them in Settings; Settings shows all six ways to follow the playhead (Look ahead and Push at the edge too) and the 'Follow again 3 s' switch; the visualizer's own buttons and Settings stay in step.
MIDI controllers, step 2: the Controllers window (menu) is now the full mappings page: see the last message a controller sent, filter by device, set a range, knob kind and speed per mapping, switch feedback, learn a mapping again, and choose the defaults for new mappings.
