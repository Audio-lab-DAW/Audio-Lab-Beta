New: the frequency map (command list: Frequency map). One picture of the whole song: time across, pitch from 20 Hz to 20 kHz up, every spot in the colour of the track that is loudest there, with the sections on top. Point at a spot to read it, click to jump there and select that track, or show one track or group glowing over the rest. It measures in the background the first time and remembers it.
Behind the scenes for the frequency map: AudioLab can now measure every track beat by beat (how loud it is in each band from 20 Hz to 20 kHz, in the middle and at the sides, loudness, brightness and its strongest notes), quietly in the background and remembered between sessions. The map that shows it comes next.
MIDI controllers, step 5: Session mode on your Ableton Push 1. Press Session: the pads show 8 tracks across and the song's sections down, lit where a track plays something; the buttons on the right (and the pads) launch a section: from a stop it plays from there, while playing it jumps at the next bar (or at the end of the section, or at once: the Launch setting on the Controllers page; Shift+button: at once). Press the waiting section again to cancel; Note goes back.
Glass for the transport and the device panel: both now float a little in from the window edges with rounded corners, so you see the arrangement around them; the bottom-left corner is properly frosted again, and the transport gets the lens, with a little extra bend on its top and bottom edges.
