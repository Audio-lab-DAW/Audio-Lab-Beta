Display-first devices lab

Six AudioLab devices at real size with the picked behaviour: the eye opens and closes the glass rail; closed, a device shows hover reveal, or on the picture (right-click the eye). Here the glass is drawn by the AudioLab glass engine, with each display canvas fed to it as a flat picture. Use it to see what floating glass over a display costs before it goes into the app.

What to look at. With "Still" displays the readout should show 0 uploads and 0 blur rebuilds per second in every state: glass over a display that does not change costs nothing. With "Live" every visible display is repainted and handed to the engine each frame; that is the worst case (ten devices all animating).

Measure runs every combination of glass, displays and state for two seconds each and makes a table to copy. Keep the window in front and the mouse still while it runs.

Limits. The displays are redrawn stand-ins, not the app's drawing code, so the "display paint" column is not the app's cost. The engine's CPU time is only the time spent in its calls; the graphics card's share shows up as frames per second.
