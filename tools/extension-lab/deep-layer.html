Mark each tool Yes, Later or No; the twelve decisions underneath already stand on my recommendation.

The mock has 6 tracks, one clip with 16 notes, a transport bar and a mixer. The ids, the settings without a normal control and the script functions are proposals to show the idea; the real lists come out of the code when the app is prepared (step DL1 in the plan). The limiter light, the record light and the master meter cannot be hidden or changed by any tool here, on purpose. Scripts here are Workshop scripts: they edit the project from outside the sound engine. They are a different thing from the script devices of prototype 3, which make sound.
