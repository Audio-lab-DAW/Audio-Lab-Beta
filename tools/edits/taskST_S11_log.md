## 28 November 2026, vVER: settings step S11, audio devices in the picked design B2: role cards and the Listening and Recording setups (task ST-S11)
Eleventh step of the settings rebuild (`docs/settings-round-1.md`, S11, depends on B (picked: B2) and S10, both done: "Audio devices in the picked design (B): role cards, the Listening and Recording setups, the proposal on arming"), with the user's answers of 27 November: (1) AudioLab may switch to Recording by itself when a track is armed, with a short quiet notice and a one-click way back; (2) Mac and Windows equally; (4) switching between setups and known devices is instant, with only a click-free ramp of a few tens of milliseconds. Lane B (settings, audio devices). Written and tested on v0.314.

### Built
- **Devices, the first card on Settings › Audio**: a switch between the two setups, **Listening** and **Recording**, and one sentence that explains what is in use ("Recording: Zoom AMS-24 records and plays, so input and output share one clock and stay in step"; with two devices it says honestly that they have two clocks and that one device for both is steadier; with no interface found it says recording uses the system input).
- **Role cards** "Listen through" and "Record from": the device's name, its kind icon and chips from the Audio devices window (profile, rate, channels, System default), and the one output select and the one input select of the app (the same elements, ids and handlers, moved: still one copy each). In the Recording setup each card says "filled automatically" or "chosen by you". The output list now shows the device that really plays (it showed System default until it was opened).
- **The Recording setup fills itself**: the input that looks most like an audio interface (the device profiles, the kind read from the label, an output of the same device, USB), never the built-in microphone, a webcam, Bluetooth or a virtual device when an interface is there; and that same device's output when it has one (matched by the browser's groupId, or by name: "Microphone (Zoom AMS-24)" and "Speakers (Zoom AMS-24)" on Windows, "Zoom AMS-24 (1686:0428)" on a Mac), so input and output run on one clock. Anything can be changed by hand while Recording is on; "Fill Recording automatically again" forgets the hand choices. The Listening setup is the device you listen through (an older profile's output). A change of output made anywhere (this card, the Audio devices window, Keep?'s Go back) is kept in the setup in use.
- **Arming**: arming an audio track switches to Recording by itself before its input is opened, so the track listens to the right device at once; a quiet notice at the lower right says what it switched to, with **Back to Listening** and a close button, and hides after 7 s. When the last such track is disarmed AudioLab goes back to Listening by itself and says so, but only when it was AudioLab that switched (a setup chosen by hand stays). A new setting, "Switch to Recording when I arm": an audio track (recommended), any track (instruments too), or never. Instruments need no input, so the recommended setting leaves them alone (lane J's Playing buffer already makes them fast while armed). Nothing switches during a recording.
- **Instant, click-free switching**: no question for setups (they are chosen on purpose, answer 1 and 4); the output is ramped down over 25 ms, switched, and ramped up over 30 ms to exactly its level before (1, or the dim level), and not ramped at all when the engine is not running. A device not seen before is remembered as known. Armed audio tracks reopen on a new input at once (they kept the old one until re-armed). The 1.5 s fade for a new or returning device and the safe volume are S13.
- **DAC and audio interface**: Record from is a read-out there with "Change on Audio" (Interface mode), like S10's read-outs.
- **Table and search**: the input row is now "Input device" on Audio, Devices (old words "record from", "microphone", "inputs" kept); new rows "Setup in use" (resettable to Listening) and "Switch to Recording when I arm" (resettable to an audio track), with help lines; "listen through", "recording setup", "arm" find them.
- Stored: `UI.st11` (the setups: which is in use, Listening's output and input, Recording's hand choices or automatic) and `UI.st11arm`. `UI.sink` and `UI.inDev` stay the live output and input as before; an older profile's input becomes the Recording setup's input, its output the Listening setup's. No key moved or renamed.
- **Mac and Windows**: the same code reads Chromium's lists on both: the system's "Default" and Windows' "Communications" entries are left out, Windows' "Speakers (...)" and "Microphone (...)" names are matched by the part in brackets and shortened to the device's name where one device does both, a Mac's USB ids are ignored. Nothing here needs the desktop helper; WASAPI exclusive and ASIO stay "needs the desktop helper" (S10's Running now).
