The pattern. The apps people call fast all have one key or one click to get back to the last project, and none make you look through a dialog to get to an empty one. Blender's splash is the closest to what you described: the splash itself holds recent files and new-file templates, and a click outside closes it. Logic and Cubase let you choose what happens at start, with the chooser or hub as one option.
Taken into all four layouts. Enter opens the most recent project (Blender, Logic's Open most recent). Typing goes straight into search (Figma, Bitwig). Arrows move through the list. ⌘N makes an empty project, ⌘O opens a file. With no projects yet, Enter makes an empty project, so a new user is never stuck.
