Readability of the version

Every rule, worst pair shown

Each rule checks its token against every surface it can sit on, including glass over the brightest and darkest clip. The worst pair is shown; the count says how many pairs were checked. Text needs both the WCAG ratio and the APCA level; controls and graphics need 3 : 1 (WCAG 1.4.11).

Track colours under colour-vision deficiency

Smallest CIEDE2000 difference between any two of the eight track colours, simulated with Machado 2009. 10 and up is colour-blind friendly, under 6 fails.

Meaning colours kept apart

Selection, record, playhead and the accent must never be mistaken for each other (CIEDE2000).

Glass

The floor is the lowest tint at which main text, labels and quiet text still pass over the brightest clip behind the glass. The glass engine draws all glass (refraction, edge, blur); a theme only sets the tint colour and its strength, never below the floor. Nothing here uses backdrop-filter.

Round 2

What changed in the rules and the tokens

Seven more rules than round 1

  • Filled buttons are measured on their own token and against the panel they sit on. In dark themes one blue could not be both a readable line and a fill for white text; splitting it closes today's white-on-blue miss without changing the blue you see on lines and arcs.
  • Clip edges must keep 3 : 1 on the selected lane too.
  • Clip titles are a rule now (4.5 : 1 and Lc 60 on the title band, all three lanes). Today's app reaches Lc 44 on its green clips.
  • Play and "on" green on panels: round 1 only measured green inside displays, and it was too pale on paper.
  • Tooltip text and its outline.
  • Halation: main text in a dark theme is never pure white.
  • Glass: the tint is at or above the measured floor.

New tokens

  • accentFill filled buttons, okS green on panels, selFill the selection's fill (a yellow highlighter in the paper themes, so yellow still means selected), tip tipInk tipEdge tooltips.
  • Style tokens, the only non-colour things a theme may set: capitals for section labels, the numeral face, the title face, dashed minor lines, clip outline width, the strength of the clip title band, the strength of the minor grid.
  • Two extra faces would ship with the app for the type accents: IBM Plex Mono (Space Race read-outs) and Source Serif 4 (Chart paper and Patent drawing titles). Body text stays Inter everywhere.

Kept from round 1

  • Device displays stay dark wells in every theme, the light ones included.
  • Meanings never change: record and over are red, units amber, ok green, selection yellow (only H1 moves it, because its accent is gold).
  • Glass thickens before text gets whiter. In round 2 quiet text may rise a little first, so most dark versions sit at 60 to 70 % instead of 80 to 85 %.
  • Appearance pairs: E2 and J2 each carry a second half for following the system's light or dark setting.
