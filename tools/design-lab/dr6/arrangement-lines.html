Today draws its lines at a width of 1 or 1.5 CSS px. At 1.75x that is 1.75 or 2.6 device pixels, so each line smears over two or three columns with a soft edge (visible in the top middle loupe on the four-bar line, the playhead and the separators). The designs draw every line in whole device pixels: a hairline is one device pixel, the playhead 2 px at 1x and 3 px at 1.75x and 2x, dotted lines use whole-pixel dots aligned to the same rows, and hairlines get 1.25 times the strength at 1.75x and above so they read the same as at 1x.

Cost

lines are drawn once per view change, never per frame

Grid, ruler, separators, clips and edges go into one picture that is redrawn only when the view, the song or a setting changes. Each display frame only copies that picture and draws the playhead on top. The read-out under the stage shows both numbers live on your machine. The same rule is proposed for the app (section 6 of the doc): today drawArr redraws the whole arrangement every playing frame when Follow is on.

Research in short

full notes with all sources: docs/design-grid-lines.md

What good grids do

  • Whisper, don't scream. People set grid lines between alpha 0.1 (faintest usable) and 0.45 (too strong); 0.2 suits every case they tested, and denser grids should be fainter. Bartram & Stone
  • Minimum spacing. REAPER hides grid lines closer than 20 px by default and you snap only to lines you see; Blender's 2D editors need 50 px between major lines. Sound On Sound, Blender
  • Adaptive grids. Ableton, Bitwig, Cubase, Studio One, Logic and FL all change the grid with zoom; Ableton shows the grid value next to the ruler; Logic numbers bars in steps of 1, 4, 8 or 16. Live manual, Logic manual
  • Fade, don't pop. Infinite-grid shaders choose the level from the log of the zoom and cross-fade the next one; Ableton's 4-bar shading that jumps while zooming is a known complaint. Ableton forum
  • Rank by alpha, length and dash. Cubase users asked for separate bar and beat colours back; matplotlib's minor ticks are about 57 % of the major length; scope graticules put minor ticks only on the centre axes. Steinberg forum, matplotlib, Teledyne LeCroy
  • Crisp hairlines. Draw in device pixels; a 1 CSS px line at 1.75x covers 1.75 device pixels and blurs. web.dev

Today in the app (v0.228, measured on the Fractals song)

  • Grid: quarter-beat lines only from 28 px per beat, then the step doubles until lines are 8 px apart, so whole levels appear and vanish at once (pop).
  • Strength: 4-bar lines 0.15 at 1.5 px, bars 0.10, beats 0.055, sixteenths 0.03, all white; Grid brightness scales them.
  • Ruler: only bar numbers with a full-height line; no beat or sixteenth ticks. Zoomed to one bar, the ruler is empty apart from the first number.
  • Track separators: a darker 1 px line plus alternating lane shades. Loop edges are already device-pixel hairlines; the selection outline and the playhead are not.
  • Each playing frame redraws the whole arrangement (Follow on).
