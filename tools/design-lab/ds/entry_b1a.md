### What changed
- **Same entry points**: Add instrument, Change instrument, the Effects Browser and Change effect open the same sheet; in the AudioLab look it is now a catalogue. Today's plane of large live cards is one click away (**Cards** in the top bar, **List** to come back; remembered in `UI.ibView`). The other looks keep today's browser.
- **Top bar**: the title, a search field that has the focus when the browser opens (finds by name, description, kind, category, tag and preset name; a row found by a preset says "Preset: …"), the keys line and Cards.
- **Categories** on the left with counts that follow the search (All, Synths, Bass & acid, …; for effects Dynamics, Delay & space, …).
- **Rows (BR2)**: logo, name, what it is for, up to three tag colours (Lead, Bass, Pad, Pluck, Keys, Drums, Effects and risers, Vocal: from the instrument's own kind and what its presets are made for; effects show their category), kind. In Change the track's sound says "On the track before".
- **Preview (B3, BP1)**: name, kind and current preset; the preview phrase's waveform on a dark well with a time scale (a tick per beat, longer per bar, seconds at each bar), rendered once offline per sound, phrase and tempo (`OfflineAudioContext`, the instrument through `synth`, an effect with the test sound through `mkFxNode`) and kept (32 sounds); key, tempo, length and peak (`Key A minor · 145 BPM · 2 bars · 3.310 s · peak −4.5 dBFS`); the presets in their groups; **In tempo, next bar** (on by default: while the song plays the preview starts on the next bar, stopped it starts at once); ▶ plays again; the one live device of the chosen sound, scaled to fit, as editable as the card was.
- **Keys**: Up and Down choose and play (the focus stays in the search field), Left and Right step the presets and play (in the search field only when it is empty), Enter adds, uses or keeps, Escape in the search field clears it first, then closes as before. Double-click a row adds, as a double-click on a card did.
- **Scrub line**: a blue line with the time moves over the waveform only while the preview plays (requestAnimationFrame from the start to the end of the phrase, then nothing); waveform and scale are drawn on change only.
- The catalogue keeps the id `#ibPlane` and the `.ibc[data-k]` rows, and the preview's live box comes first in the DOM, so the Change code (swap on one click, Escape puts the old one back, Keep), `ibLiveRender`, double-click and Enter work unchanged. The plane's layout, zoom and pan stay off the catalogue.
