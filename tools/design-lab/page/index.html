The rules every option follows

  • Subtle, as now. Hairlines and measurement details only; no drawings or motion on working surfaces. The only things that move are what already moves (playhead, meters, clock).
  • Three line strengths, like a scope graticule. Bar, beat and sixteenth lines at three fixed strengths, so the eye finds the bar without counting.
  • True values, with units. Every read-out is a real number of the project (145 BPM: one bar is 1.655 s), digits of equal width, mono only for time.
  • Blue never as a hairline. Fine lines and small marks use a paler blue or white; the blue stays for large fills (NASA colour guidance: blue carries little detail).
  • Glass rules kept. No backdrop-filter anywhere; the glass engine draws the glass. Options only change what is drawn on top of the panels. In Light mode the same marks are drawn on solid panels and nothing animates that does not have to.
