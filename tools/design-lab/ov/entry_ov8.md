### What changed
- **⌘K (Ctrl+K) opens the command palette** at the top of the window, over every command of the registry (`xtCommands()`: the shortcut list `SCA` with lanes C and D's `ctl.*`, `ni.*`, `clip.*`, `arr.*` and the extra commands count-in, tap tempo and capture; gestures such as Copy or Shift-drag are left out since they cannot be run). Fuzzy search (a word start or the first letter counts more, gaps less), the matched letters in blue, the key on the right in quiet mono. With nothing typed: **Recent** first (the last five commands run from the palette, kept in the settings as `UI.ovRecent`), then the groups of Settings › Keyboard shortcuts (Transport, Editing, Windows & view, Project & files, Help & tools).
- **The Assistant inside**: while something is typed, the last row is "Ask the Assistant: “…”" (opens the Assistant and sends it). **⌘K again** opens the Assistant with what was typed, not sent, so the old ⌘K is one press further. The Assistant's row in the shortcut list has no default key now; a key the user gave it is kept, and every other way to open it (the button by the logo) is unchanged.
- **The ? key sheet**: ? in the empty palette, or ? anywhere outside a text field (a new shortcut row, changeable in Settings), shows every shortcut in two columns by area, with a search that also finds keys ("shift" lists the Shift keys), the number of keys and of keys in conflict (amber), and Change keys in Settings. Keys that are words read "Right-click + Drag".
- Two new rows in the shortcut list and in `docs/commands.md`: `help.palette` (Mod+K) and `help.keySheet` (?). Nothing is built at start: the rows join `SCA` after the script has run, the registry and the palette are made on the first ⌘K.
- Keyboard and screen readers: the search is a combobox with `aria-activedescendant` over a listbox; arrows (wrapping), Page Up/Down, Enter runs, Esc closes and gives focus back to where it was, Tab stays inside, a click outside closes. Glass from the engine (class `pop`, drawn as a menu face; solid at the Solid level), no backdrop-filter; a 90 ms fade, none with reduced motion, Motion "Signal only" or in Light mode. Hook `OV_HOOKS.palette` (open, close, keys) for LM2 (Help menu) and lane M.
