### Built (block "lane E (task IN-G)" before `askChoice`)
- **G1, one drawer `INS.grh`** (FabFilter Pro-L style, as on the pick page): 4 s of history, the input as a grey area, the output as a blue line (`#4c8dff`), the gain reduction in red hanging from the top **on the same dB scale** (1 dB down is 1 dB), the level scale (dBFS) on the left and the GR scale on the right in the GR colour, the GR now as a big number with its amber unit (small capitals, MD1's M1) and the most in the last 4 s under it, a small IN OUT GR legend. One point per device pixel column (highest input and output, deepest reduction), so a narrow display does not draw hundreds of points.
  - **Compressor** (`vizComp`, now one call to `ingComp`): its history beside the curve, scale 0 to −30 dBFS (deeper when the threshold is lower), the threshold as an amber line. The output is the input plus the reduction plus **Chromium's own make-up** (the DynamicsCompressor adds 0.6 x the reduction at 0 dBFS; measured: the drawn output is within 0.33 dB of the real one) plus Make-up. In the device panel the history is a narrow strip, so the numbers sit in the curve's empty top-left corner there.
  - **Limiter**: on its own −30 to +3 dB scale (the ceiling handle and the input and release dots keep their places), dB scales on both sides, the numbers in the right margin; the GR and OUT bars without numbers are gone (G1 replaces them). Its history already held 5.5 s.
  - **Gate**: on its −80 to 0 dB scale; the green open shading, the open and close lines, the OPEN mark and the envelope panel stay. The output is the level plus the gate's gain.
  - **Ducker** (`abxPaint`, also the Auto gain display while live): the trigger stays a filled area in its track's colour; the reduction is now in dB in red on the trigger's 60 dB scale (it was the linear gain as a line), with the GR scale on the right and the 4 s maximum under the existing read-out.
- **G3, the compressor's transfer curve, calibrated:** −60 to 0 dBFS both ways, a line every 6 dB, labels every 12 with real minus signs, IN dBFS, the 1:1 line dashed, the threshold dashed red. **The curve is the one Chromium's compressor really computes** (`ingCK`, `ingRed`, a port of DynamicsCompressorKernel: an exponential knee in linear amplitude starting at the threshold, its constant found so the slope at threshold + 6 dB is 1/ratio, then the ratio; the old drawing had no knee). The signal rides it as a white dot at the input's peak (the compressor's detector reads the peak; measured) and the reduction the compressor reports, with a 30-point trail (none at the Light level) and an **amber caliper** to the 1:1 line with the reduction's number. The curve is computed once per setting.
- **G4, `ingBar`**: a bar per band falling from the top on the studio scale 0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 10, 15, 20, 30 dB (square-root spacing: the first dB gets 18 % of the bar), falling 25 dB/s with a white tick held 2 s (`INS.peak` from IN-A), the number under it, the band's edges:
  - **OTT**: each band's level block stays (narrower) with its bar beside it, the edges "< 120 Hz", "120 Hz to 2.5 kHz", "> 2.5 kHz" (the fixed crossovers);
  - **Multiband Dynamics**: the right-hand GR meter (a linear 18 dB bar) becomes three G4 bars with the edges from its crossovers ("<150", "150-3k", ">3k"), or mid and side;
  - **De-Esser**: the reduction panel becomes the history on the same scale with a G4 bar, and the band's real edges (the worklet's band-pass: Q 1.5 at 6.5 kHz is 4.7 to 9.0 kHz).
- **Display history kept 4 s:** the gate's, the de-esser's and the ducker's message lists keep 400 messages of 512 samples instead of 200 (display arrays only; the ducker's ring is in the AB code, a one-number change).
- **Not changed:** the audio path (no node made or connected; the values are the reductions and worklet messages the devices already post); the hit tests and handles; the Transient Shaper (not in the picks). The compressor's GR bar beside the curve stays as it was.
- **Overlaps named:** lane L (SND, built-in sounds, AE4 picks) may change the Compressor (B26 detector, AE3's X1 sidechain menu): if it changes the curve or the detector, `ingCK` and the live point need the same change (one place).
