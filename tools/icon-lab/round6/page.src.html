K6: K5a with real rings (tapered, never flat)

Each ring is a round tube on a circle in space, tilted 65 degrees, seen through a camera. Where it comes toward you it is thick and white, where it goes away it is thin and dim, and at every crossing the nearer ring passes in front. The three rings are nested like a gyroscope (3.5 % apart), so they never run through each other. K5a's soft glass, glow and field are kept. Dashed: K5a as you picked it, flat.

E6: M5d as the Earth (yellow) and the Moon (blue)

The field of M5d is the Earth's magnetic field: a dipole, each line r = L sin²θ. Here every line lies in a real plane through the magnetic axis; the right side comes toward you, the left goes away behind the Earth. Dashed: M5d as you picked it, flat.
