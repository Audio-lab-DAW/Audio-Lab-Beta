AudioLab app icon, round 3: dipole field

From your picks: I7 dipole field … P2 Orbit Night … P8 Chart Paper. Just make sure that the field in the center should be centered with a circle … the field should be perfectly around that as if it's the Earth. Let's also use the porthole … and W7 with measurements … I do like the fine particles.

The fix first: in round 2 the field was centred on the tile, but the shipped atom carries a small optical offset, so the nucleus sat 10 px left and 20 px up of the field's centre. Now each design puts the nucleus first and draws the field lines (r = L sin²θ) around that exact point; every line starts and ends on a circle a clean gap outside the yellow nucleus, like field lines on the Earth. Inside the rings only the innermost loops are drawn; the outer lines come out from behind the atom. Switch each design between P2 and P8, open it on the bench, press Pick, then paste the line from the bar.
