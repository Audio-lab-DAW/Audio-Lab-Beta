AudioLab built-in sounds: what to improve and add

I measured every built-in instrument and the main effects of AudioLab v0.263 through the app's own render, headless: aliasing on high notes, filter and EQ curves, envelope speed, stereo, compressor behaviour, reverb tails and cost. Then I compared each one with the plug-ins you own (Serum 2, Phase Plant, Pigments, Kick 3, SubLabXL, FabFilter, Kilohearts, Valhalla) and with what full-on psytrance needs most.

Each card gives the device a grade for today and numbered improvements (B1, B2 ...). New devices are N1 to N6. Any change that alters the sound is a v2 engine mode or a new device, so old projects never change by themselves. Tick what you want and paste the line from the bar at the bottom. The play buttons compare today (rendered by the app) with a prototype computed in this page on the same notes.

The audit in numbers v0.263, 48 kHz, headless Chromium; script tools/engine-lab/ae4/measure.py

Aliasing is the energy between 40 Hz and 0.45 × the fundamental of a single held C7 (2093 Hz), against the whole sound. A clean high note has nothing there, so detune and unison cannot fake it. Cost is offline render time per second of audio, minus an empty render: a rough guide, not a live CPU meter.
