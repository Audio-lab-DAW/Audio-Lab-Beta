Fired by the scheduler. Built on 28 Nov while the release gate's freeze (09:11:57 UTC) held index.html, on top of SQ1 (released as v0.296 just before). Edit script `tools/engine-lab/sq/sq3.py` (inserts `sq3.js` right after SQ1's block; re-runnable, asserted anchors), test `tools/ui-tests/taskSQ3.py` (45 checks).

### Built
- With Hi-Fi on for the song (`S.sq==='clean'`), a buffer played at another rate than its own is converted once to that rate with a windowed-sinc resampler (Kaiser, 32 zero crossings, beta 10, cut-off at 0.96 of the lower Nyquist; a polyphase bank of exact phases, each normalised to a sum of 1), in a worker, and cached per rate (a WeakMap on the original buffer). Covered: the built-in sampler and drum sounds (44.1 kHz), bounces (44.1 kHz), the stem splitter's results (44.1 kHz) and its input, songs saved at another rate, and every clip when an export runs at another rate.
- The hook: `AudioBufferSourceNode.prototype.buffer`'s setter (`sqRsPick`). Hi-Fi off passes the very same buffer: today's linear path, bit for bit. Live, a buffer without its copy yet plays as before and is converted in the worker for the next time; copies start when a buffer is loaded or made (`BUFS.set`, `SBUF.set`) and for all loaded buffers when the song turns Hi-Fi on or a Hi-Fi song opens.
- Exports wait for the copies: `renderMix`, `renderMixChunked` (lane K's, wrapped only), `bounceRender`, `neuTo44`; a buffer made during an export (a drum sound on demand) is converted on the spot by the same function (same bits as the worker), so no render depends on timing. Other offline renders (the map's analysis) never convert on the main thread outside an export.
- The Hi-Fi row's tooltip (Settings > Project) says it. Hooks: `SQ_HOOKS.resample(buffer, rate)`, `SQ_HOOKS.resampleNow`, `SQ_HOOKS.warm(rate)`.

### What NB must port
- The resampler, bit for bit: U/D = out rate / in rate reduced by their gcd; fc = min(1, out/in) * 0.96; W = ceil(32/fc), T = 2W; phase ph's taps t at d = ph/U + (W-1-t): sinc(fc*d) * I0(10*sqrt(1-(fc*d/32)^2)) / I0(10) when |fc*d| < 32, else 0 (sin from fdlibm as V8; I0 the plain series, at most 63 terms, stop when a term is under 1e-21 of the sum); each phase divided by its own sum (double). Output i reads input j = k-W+1+t (0 outside), sum in double in tap order without FMA, rounded to float once; k and ph advanced in integers (ph += D; k += floor(ph/U); ph -= floor(ph/U)*U). Output length ceil(n*U/D).
- When: only in a Hi-Fi song (`sq` 'clean'), for a buffer played at another rate; exports convert every buffer first; Hi-Fi off plays the original buffer (Chromium's linear interpolation, already ported).
