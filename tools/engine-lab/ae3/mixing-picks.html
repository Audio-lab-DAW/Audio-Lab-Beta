AudioLab mixing: workflow and clarity

You named two pains in a big psytrance project: latency when playing, and mixing clarity (hearing and seeing what clashes, kick and bass, loudness). I read AudioLab's mixer code (v0.263) and the plans already running (the 40 feature cards, the 28 ideas and your meter picks), then looked at how Bitwig, Live, Cubase, Logic, Studio One, REAPER and FL Studio handle mixing, at clarity tools (Neutron, Pro-Q 4, Trackspacer, soothe2, REFERENCE, Metric AB) and at how psytrance producers lock kick and bass.

These 27 options cover what is not planned yet, and improve planned cards where the research shows how. Each one says whether it is new, improves a card or idea already being built, or is covered. X2, X15 and X16 play sound: press Play, then drag or switch.

The live cards synthesize a 145 BPM kick (tail on F1, 43.65 Hz) and a rolling bass in F in the page itself; the clash meter uses third-octave band filters with a 10 ms envelope and counts a clash when both tracks are within 12 dB of each other and within 35 dB of the loudest band at the same moment.
