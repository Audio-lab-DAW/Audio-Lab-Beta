Where the milliseconds go

A key press, today and after

Drawn to scale, 0 to 60 ms. Main-thread, start and delay-compensation parts are measured on the build machine; the device part on your Mac is estimated from a published Chrome 153 loop-back on an M4 and the Gryphon's reported 14 + 14 frames.

Through the Mac's default output in your reports (Bose via eqMac at 16 kHz) the device part alone is 82 ms plus the Bluetooth link, usually 100 to 200 ms more. Check Settings › Audio when you play.
