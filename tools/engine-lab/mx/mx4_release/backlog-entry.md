### Built
- **X3, the Ducker's Spectral focus**: "Spectral (32 bands)" in the Ducker's Listen to (device menu and both layouts of the sidechain window, with a tooltip), and a preset "Bass under kick (spectral)". 32 bands from 20 Hz to 20 kHz (third-octave-like); in each band the track goes down only as much as the trigger is there and only while it sounds, with the Ducker's own Duck amount, Trigger level, Trigger gain, Attack, Hold, Release and Curve. Built in the Ducker's worklet as a short-time Fourier transform (Hann in and out, 75 % overlap, both channels packed in one transform; unity when nothing ducks). Latency one frame (1024 samples at 44.1 and 48 kHz, 2048 above 64 kHz) plus the look-ahead, reported by `fxLat`, so MX-2 lines up every other track, live and in export. The other four focus modes run exactly the code they ran before. The Focus dial is hidden for Spectral (it has no single frequency).
- **X19, Bass mono**: a switch "Bass mono 120 Hz" in each track's first device (under Mono) and above the master's devices. Click to turn on or off; click the number or right-click for 50, 80, 100, 120, 150, 200, 300 or 500 Hz; one undo step. Below the frequency the side goes away: the side through a Linkwitz-Riley high-pass of 48 dB per octave, the mid through the matching all-pass, so a mono signal keeps its level at every frequency. Live it sits inside the track's utility (after width and phase, before the trim) and at the start of the master chain; `renderMix` builds the same. A frequency change moves the same filters (no rebuild). Data `t.bmono = {on, f}` and `S.bmono` (optional: songs without them build nothing).
- **Hooks** (`MX_HOOKS`): `bandFilter(ctx, lo, hi, order)` (Linkwitz-Riley band, order 4 or 8, `{in, out}`) for lane E's band listen (X17) and the low-band correlation (X19's meter and warning); `bassMono(t)` and `setBassMono(t, on, f)` (the master as `{kind:'master'}`); `specBands(t, f)` (the 32 band gains of a Spectral Ducker while playing, for lane E's drawing) and `specEdges()`.
