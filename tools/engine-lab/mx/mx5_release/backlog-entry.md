### Built
- **Dual Path Latency**, off by default (`UI.mxDpl`), turned on in Settings, Audio (a card of its own; the row `mxDpl` in the settings table, stable name `audio.dualPath`, the switch `data-al="settings.dualPath"`), or with the command `mx.dualPath`. It works on the track you play, by lane J's rule (armed, or the selected instrument while the computer keyboard plays, and lane J's played-track exception on: `liveTracks()`, `UI.ltPdc`). That track keeps its slow devices and does not wait for them:
  - look-ahead devices run a quick stand-in while you play: the Limiter with 1 sample of look-ahead, the Gate without look-ahead, the Soft Clipper without oversampling;
  - a latent device with a Mix knob (Drive, Vocoder) plays its dry part at once and its wet part when it is ready: two paths, the device at full wet scaled by Mix and the dry part around it scaled by 1 - Mix, both following the knob.
  The stand-ins are copies handed to the device's own `set()`; `f.p` is never changed. Tracks you do not play, playback when you do not play, and every export (renderMix builds its own nodes from `f.p`) use the exact version. With the switch off nothing is built: the wrapper passes `f.p` through untouched and the chain is wired as before; clicks and keys do no work for it.
- **The mark, subtle (the user's picks):** next to the name in Settings a small grey tag "AudioLab Pro Technology" with the atom mark (tooltip: "AudioLab Pro Technology. Other DAWs turn slow devices off to cut latency; Dual Path Latency keeps them and brings them back in sync."); the first time it is turned on, one first-use card inside the Settings card (a small drawing: the fast part at once, the slow part joining in time, playback and export exact; the user's line; "Got it", never shown again); the About card gets a small "AudioLab Pro Technology" section before "Under the hood": Dual Path Latency, designed by Ilja V. - A line under the switch says what it does for the track you play now (its devices' delay with Dual Path Latency against without), updated when Settings opens and when the switch moves, never per frame.
- Hook `MX_HOOKS.dpl`: `on(t)`, `lat(t)` (samples while you play), `name`, `tag`; for lane E's meters or lane I's title strip if they want to show the number while playing.
